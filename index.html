About the Founder

Our journey began during our founder's time at Lloyd's, where deep technical grounding in thermal insulation systems was gained. Relocating to Aurangabad, the rapidly growing industrial base and the rising demand for insulation, civil works, and acoustics inspired the establishment of Sai Enterprises.

We became the first dedicated insulation company in the Marathwada region, and today our hands-on, detail-oriented leadership has delivered 500+ projects pan-India — from complex high-difficulty industrial jobs to long-term AMC contracts.
