🎨 Color Palette
Primary Accent
Crimson Red (#D31018 / #CC0000): Used for primary high-priority call-to-actions (CTAs) like GET A QUOTE and LEARN MORE, active navigation tab highlights, key underline accents, and stats box borders.

Secondary & Tint Accents
Dusty Rose Tint (#E2B2B2): Used as a soft background highlight for active or hovered feature cards (e.g., the Thermal Insulation card).

Communication Accents:

Electric Blue (#007AFF): Phone contact action button.

WhatsApp Green (#25D366): Floating chat widget.

Neutrals & Backgrounds
Charcoal / Off-Black (#1A1A1A / #222222): Applied to dark contrasting sections (such as Our Latest Projects and stats banner) and primary dark text headers.

Pure White (#FFFFFF): Main body background and content card backgrounds.

Medium Gray (#666666): Secondary body text, descriptions, and inactive menu tab backgrounds.

✒️ Typography
Font Style: Clean, modern, geometric Sans-Serif (e.g., Montserrat or Poppins).

Headings:

Hero Header: Bold, high-contrast, uppercase text overlaid on imagery.

Section Titles: Bold, high-weight title casing with tight line spacing.

Body Text: Standard weight, easy-to-read dark gray/white depending on section contrast, styled with high line height for clarity.

🧩 UI Components & Layout Features
1. Navigation & Header
Clean white sticky top navbar with drop-down menus (Products, Application, INSUDESIGN, etc.).

Standout red call-to-action button placed top-right (GET A QUOTE →).

2. Feature Cards & Iconography
Minimalist line-art icons paired with short descriptive blocks for key service areas (Thermal, Acoustic, Fire, Thermo-Acoustic).

Interactive cards with subtle light-to-tinted hover states.

3. Project Portfolio Filter Bar
Dark contrast background with a horizontal tabbed menu (Commercial, Educational, Healthcare, Residential, etc.).

Active state highlighted in solid crimson red with a wrench icon prefix.

4. Impact & Stats Counter
Dark charcoal block with white bold numeric stats, subtle red corner accents (L-shaped borders), and light secondary text.

📐 Overall Aesthetic & Vibe
Industrial & Architectural: Structured grid, high-contrast dark-and-light layout suitable for an engineering/construction brand.

Trustworthy & Clean: Uses balanced whitespace, strong corporate reds, and clear visual hierarchy to guide visitors toward requesting a quote.
