PUF Panels & Sections

Polyurethane foam insulation for cold storage, clean rooms & ducting

What are PUF Panels?

PUF (polyurethane foam) panels and pipe sections are among the most energy-efficient insulation products for low-temperature applications. Sai Enterprises manufactures PUF pipe sections in-house and supplies PUF panels for cold storage, clean rooms and ducting systems.

With in-house manufacturing capability and strong dealerships, we deliver competitive pricing and fast supply across Aurangabad, Nashik, Pune, Mumbai and all of India.

Typical Uses

  • • Hot pipelines in process & chemical plants
  • • Boilers, storage tanks & heat exchangers
  • • Furnace & kiln insulation
  • • Air ducts & HVAC hot water lines
  • • Acoustic absorption behind cladding
